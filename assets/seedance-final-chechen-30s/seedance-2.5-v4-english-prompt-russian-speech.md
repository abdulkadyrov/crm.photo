# Seedance 2.5 — 30-second vertical advertisement

## Upload the reference images in this exact order

1. Female photographer in a hijab and long dress
2. Kindergarten photo session with one child
3. The same child as a superhero
4. A girl as a fairy-tale princess
5. A child giving an interview in kindergarten
6. A grown child watching the childhood recording with parents

## Main prompt

Create one polished 30-second vertical advertising film in 9:16 format, using the six uploaded reference images in their numbered order.

LANGUAGE LOCK — CRITICAL:
- All production directions in this prompt are written in English and are silent instructions only.
- Do not read or speak any English instruction aloud.
- Every quoted dialogue line must be spoken in Russian exactly as written.
- Use clear, natural, native Russian pronunciation.
- Do not translate, paraphrase, invent, or mix the dialogue with any other language.
- No English, Chinese, pseudo-language, gibberish, or unintelligible speech.
- Only the interview segment shows visible lip-sync. All other spoken lines are off-screen voice-over.

IDENTITY AND CONTINUITY:
- Preserve every person’s face, age, skin tone, hairstyle, and identity from the corresponding reference image.
- The people have authentic Chechen / North Caucasian appearance.
- Do not replace faces or change ethnicity.
- Keep the photographer modestly dressed in her long loose dress and beige hijab.
- Show only one child being photographed in the real photo-session scene.
- Do not create duplicate children, extra fingers, distorted hands, malformed faces, or changing clothes.
- The superhero and princess scenes are imaginative transformations of children’s portraits, not dangerous real events.

VISUAL STYLE:
Premium emotional family advertisement, warm natural light, realistic cinematic photography, gentle camera movement, shallow depth of field, smooth match cuts, coherent color grading, high facial fidelity, believable physics, tasteful magical effects. The opening is energetic and magical; the ending is warm, intimate, and nostalgic. No logos, watermarks, subtitles, captions, interface text, CRM screens, or random written words.

TIMELINE:

0.0–3.2 seconds — SUPERHERO HOOK
Start with a close-up of a printed kindergarten portrait inspired by Image 2. The portrait emits a soft golden glow and transforms smoothly into Image 3. The boy becomes a confident child superhero; his cape moves gently in the wind and a warm trail of light circles behind him. Preserve his face exactly. Dynamic but safe cinematic push-in.

Russian female voice-over, warm and intrigued, spoken exactly:
«Что, если детская фотография сможет ожить?»

3.2–6.5 seconds — PRINCESS DREAM
Use a magical match cut into Image 4. The girl remains fully recognizable while her portrait comes alive in a fairy-tale garden. Her dress moves naturally, tiny warm lights float around her, and the distant castle glows at sunset. She smiles and makes one gentle turn. No dialogue from the girl.

6.5–10.5 seconds — HOW THE MEMORY BEGINS
Transition from the magical glow to the real kindergarten photo session in Image 2. Show one child sitting for a portrait while the female photographer from Image 1 photographs the child. Keep the environment bright, calm, and welcoming. Show a brief camera shutter moment and the child’s natural smile. Do not show two children posing together. Do not show a printed photograph containing two children.

Russian female voice-over, sincere and clear, spoken exactly:
«Мы сохраняем не только улыбки, но и детские мечты».

10.5–14.0 seconds — PORTRAIT BECOMES A STORY
Show two fast, elegant visual beats: the new portrait appears on the camera display, then the portrait briefly comes alive with subtle motion. Follow with very short flashes of the superhero and princess worlds. Preserve each child’s face. No dialogue.

14.0–20.0 seconds — CHILDHOOD INTERVIEW
Use Image 5. Medium close-up of one child alone, seated in a real kindergarten room. The child looks slightly beside the camera toward an unseen female educator. The educator never enters the frame; only her gentle off-screen voice is heard. Quiet room tone, clean speech, realistic pauses, precise Russian lip-sync only for the child.

Off-screen female educator asks in Russian exactly:
«Кем ты хочешь стать?»

The child smiles, thinks briefly, then answers in Russian exactly:
«Врачом. Хочу помогать людям!»

20.0–23.0 seconds — TIME PASSES
The child’s smile freezes naturally into a photograph. Use a gentle match dissolve through turning album pages and warm particles of light. Time passes from childhood to adulthood without morphing faces on screen.

23.0–30.0 seconds — THE MEMORY RETURNS
Use Image 6, but ensure the grown child and both parents have consistent Chechen / North Caucasian appearance. They sit closely together in a warm family home and watch the old kindergarten interview on a smartphone. The smartphone screen clearly shows the same child from Image 5 speaking in the kindergarten setting. Keep the screen stable and correctly oriented. Their reaction is restrained, genuine, and emotional: soft smiles and slightly tearful eyes. Finish on the family, the phone, and the open photo album.

Russian male voice-over, warm and nostalgic, spoken exactly:
«Через годы можно снова увидеть себя маленьким и услышать свой голос. Не просто фотографии — детство, в которое можно вернуться».

AUDIO:
- Natural Russian voices recorded cleanly and close to the microphone.
- Female voice-over: warm adult voice, calm advertising delivery.
- Educator: gentle adult female voice, heard off-screen only.
- Child: authentic age-appropriate voice, relaxed and clearly understandable.
- Final voice-over: warm adult male voice, emotional but restrained.
- Soft cinematic music under the speech; lower the music automatically whenever anyone speaks.
- Add subtle camera shutter, light magical shimmer, quiet kindergarten ambience, and soft album-page sounds.
- No singing, chanting, crowd speech, robotic voice, voice overlap, or invented words.

FINAL QUALITY RULE:
Prioritize correct Russian speech, stable faces, and emotional clarity over extra visual effects. If a shot becomes unstable, simplify the movement instead of changing a face or adding people.
